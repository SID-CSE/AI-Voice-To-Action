# Potential Research Contributions

These are candidate contributions, not claims of completed novelty.

1. **Task formulation:** a benchmark schema for extracting actions together with verbatim evidence, explicit unknowns, workspace-supported resolutions, and risk/confirmation labels.
2. **Evidence-grounded pipeline:** a retrieval and verification method that abstains when transcript and workspace evidence do not support an owner or deadline.
3. **Risk-aware decision policy:** a deterministic or calibrated policy for escalating consequential requests, evaluated on adversarial and benign near-miss cases.
4. **Empirical study:** a controlled quality-faithfulness-safety-latency comparison of one-shot, grounded, staged, and ablated workflows.
5. **Reproducible artifact:** sanitized benchmark fixtures, annotation guide, evaluator, prompts, configuration, and failure taxonomy.

Each contribution is conditional on implementation, comparison with prior work, and statistically supported results. Product features such as Clerk, Neon, React UI, and deployment should be described as system engineering rather than novelty.
